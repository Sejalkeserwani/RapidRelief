1. The Problem (The "Why")
In crowded cities, ambulances get stuck in traffic because drivers don't know they are coming until it’s too late. Also, bystanders often don't know how to give basic first aid while waiting, which leads to a loss of critical time (the "Golden Hour").
2. The Solution (The "What")
RapidRelief is an IoT-powered Emergency Ecosystem that connects patients, ambulances, and commuters in real-time. It has three main "brains":
For Commuters: It uses a smartphone's microphone and GPS to detect an approaching ambulance. It alerts nearby drivers to "Clear the Lane" before they even see the siren.
For Patients: It provides an instant SOS button and a step-by-step first aid guide (like CPR or bleeding control) to keep the patient safe until help arrives.
For Hospitals: It sends the patient’s live location and medical details to the doctors so they are 100% prepared before the ambulance reaches the door.
3. The Unique Edge (The "How")
Unlike a simple map, we use Signal Processing (FFT) to listen for sirens and Real-time WebSockets to ensure there is zero delay in communication between all three parties.
The "30-Second Elevator Pitch"
"RapidRelief is a smart emergency system that clears traffic for ambulances before they get stuck. By using a smartphone's sensors to 'hear' sirens and 'track' locations, we create a green-wave for the ambulance while giving bystanders life-saving first aid instructions in real-time. We are essentially turning every smartphone into a safety tool to save lives during the most critical minutes of an accident."
